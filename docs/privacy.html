Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · KeyDeck (MIT Open Source)

The developer of KeyDeck collects no personal data, analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers, and KeyDeck does not track you. There is no KeyDeck backend server or account. The only data that leaves your device is what you choose to send to a third-party AI provider, described in section 2.

1. Data Stored On Your Device

  • API Keys: Your Google Gemini and/or OpenAI API keys are stored in the iOS Shared Keychain using kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly and are never sent anywhere except directly to their respective provider.
  • Recordings & History Log: Voice recordings and transcripts are saved locally on your iPhone (encrypted at rest by iOS Data Protection) so you can retry failed network calls or copy past rambles. Swipe to delete any entry and its audio file at any time.
  • Snippets, Settings & Clipboard History: Stored locally in your private App Group container.

2. Direct Third-Party AI Requests (BYOK)

When you explicitly trigger Rambler, Voice Edit, or a Quick Tweak, your audio or text is sent directly from your iPhone over HTTPS (using ephemeral, zero-cache network sessions) to Google Gemini or OpenAI under your own API key.

If Send Surrounding Field Context is enabled (off by default; toggleable in one tap from the keyboard), nearby text may be included to improve dictation accuracy. KeyDeck never captures text from password or secure fields (isSecureTextEntry == true).

Your permission comes first. KeyDeck shows a disclosure naming Google and OpenAI and asks for your explicit permission before any data is sent. Nothing is sent until you tap Allow AI Data Sharing. You can withdraw permission at any time in KeyDeck → Settings → AI Data Sharing; while it is off, KeyDeck makes no AI requests.

What is sent: your voice recording, the text you ask KeyDeck to edit, your custom dictionary and style instructions, and (only if enabled) surrounding field text. Retention: the provider processes and may temporarily retain your request under its API terms (for example, for abuse monitoring). See Google Gemini API Additional Terms and OpenAI's Privacy Policy. KeyDeck itself never receives this data.

3. Why "Allow Full Access" Is Required

iOS requires keyboard extensions to request Allow Full Access in order to communicate with the KeyDeck companion app via App Groups (for microphone capture, snippets, and clipboard items) and to read your API keys from the Shared Keychain for Quick Tweaks. KeyDeck never logs keystrokes.

4. Deleting Your Data

Delete individual history entries by swiping in the History tab, clear API keys in Settings, or delete the app to remove all KeyDeck data from your device. Requests already sent to Google or OpenAI are managed through your account with that provider.

5. Children

KeyDeck is not directed to children under 13 and requires an API key from a third-party provider.
